History & complete rules
In Arcade, choose any occupied card. Correct guesses replace that card; wrong guesses remove it unless protected by a shield. Shield tokens can be placed on any card; one shield prevents one ordinary miss. Cash Out uses a streak of 3+ to earn another shield token and resets your streak. Peek reveals the upcoming regular card; Swap lets you exchange two occupied board positions. Push Your Luck commits to two guesses on one card for 3× combined points, but shields cannot save a missed Push. Complete a newly filled row, column, or diagonal for a line bonus; filling the same line again after it breaks earns another bonus. Jokers never appear on the board. Classic mode uses automatic board order without Arcade powers or line scoring. Ace is low; ties lose. Arcade Run: survive three consecutive decks (3, 2, then 1 starting cards) to win. Classic: clear one deck. Endless: keep clearing decks for the highest score. Each cleared deck advances the stage; lose all board cards and the run ends.
