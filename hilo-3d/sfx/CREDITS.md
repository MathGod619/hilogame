# HiLo sound credits

The following source sounds are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). HiLo uses short edited excerpts.

- [Casino Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio) by Kenney. Card slides, landings, discard, flip, shuffle, and chip handling. The named samples are from [Kenney's original archive](https://opengameart.org/content/54-casino-sound-effects-cards-dice-chips).
- [Antique cash register](https://freesound.org/people/kyles/sounds/452572/) by kyles. The Tollkeeper entrance.
- [Metal Latch](https://freesound.org/people/mm4wood/sounds/760626/) by mm4wood. The Collector entrance and slot locks.
- [Engine_Start.wav](https://freesound.org/people/CogFireStudios/sounds/421000/) by CogFireStudios. The Final Driver entrance.
- [Car Engine](https://freesound.org/people/Kraftaggregat/sounds/737642/) by Kraftaggregat. The Final Driver Joker cue.
